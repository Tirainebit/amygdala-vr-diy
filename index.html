Inside the Amygdala

A looping walk-through of trigger → amygdala → emotion, using five smells as examples. Look around: the story plays in front of you, with the smell input on your left and the emotion console below.

  1. Tap Start full screen, hold the phone up, and turn or tilt it to look around. No headset needed. On a computer, drag with the mouse.
  2. Tap once to recenter. Double-tap to bring this menu back.
  3. Using the cardboard headset? Use Start VR headset and the lens settings below.
